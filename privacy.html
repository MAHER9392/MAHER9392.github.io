Tic Tac Toe/XO - The Clean One

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Tic Tac Toe/XO - The Clean One, published by MAHER9392, handles information when the game is used. The game does not require a user account for normal gameplay.

Local game data

Game settings, preferences, scores, statistics, themes, and similar gameplay information may be stored locally on the device so the game can remember choices and progress.

Feedback and bug reports

The game may provide optional tools for sending an in-game rating, written feedback, or a bug report to the developer. When these tools are used, the submitted text may be sent together with limited technical and gameplay context to help diagnose issues and improve the game.

This context may include game mode, board size, AI difficulty, selected theme, app version, version code, device manufacturer and model, Android version, language, screen size, processor architecture, and a randomly generated installation identifier used only to distinguish reports from the same app installation.

The random installation identifier is created by the game and is not a device serial number, Android ID, phone number, email address, or advertising identifier.

Closed-testing theme submissions

During limited Google Play testing, the game may provide an optional tester-only tool for temporarily accessing premium theme controls and sending a custom theme to the developer for evaluation. When a tester chooses to use "Send Theme," the submission may include the selected theme settings, colors and styles, app version, language, board-size context, the random installation identifier described above, and a full-screen screenshot generated from the game screen so the developer can see how the theme looks in actual use.

The screenshot is sent only after the tester chooses to submit the theme. Depending on the device, visible system interface elements around the game, such as the status or navigation bars, may also appear in the screenshot. The tester-only premium access is temporary and is not treated as a Google Play purchase. This closed-testing feature may be disabled or removed before public production release.

Private review access

The game contains a private review-access mechanism that allows authorized Google Play reviewers to test premium functionality without making a purchase. When valid review access is activated, limited technical information such as app version, device model, Android version, language, screen size, processor architecture, and the random installation identifier may be sent to the developer for verification and troubleshooting. The submitted review-access code is not included in developer notifications.

Online processing

Feedback, bug reports, review-access requests, and optional closed-testing theme submissions are sent over HTTPS to a backend hosted on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare may process network and connection information as part of providing and securing its service. The backend may use connection information for short-lived abuse protection and does not forward the raw IP address to Discord.

Accepted feedback, bug reports, successful review-access notifications, and tester-submitted themes or theme screenshots may be forwarded to private developer channels on Discord so the developer can review feedback, investigate bugs, verify review access, and evaluate theme designs.

See Cloudflare Privacy Policy and Discord Privacy Policy.

Advertising

The free version of the game may show advertisements using Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads. Google and its advertising partners may process technical, advertising, interaction, and diagnostic information as part of providing, measuring, securing, and improving advertising services, subject to their own policies and applicable law.

See Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob privacy information.

In-app purchases

The game may offer optional purchases such as removing ads or unlocking premium features. Purchases are processed through Google Play. Payment processing is handled by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policies.

Offline play

Many parts of the game can be used offline. Features that depend on advertising, purchases, feedback, bug reporting, review access, tester-only theme submission, or other online services may be unavailable until an internet connection is restored.

Data retention and deletion

The game does not require an account profile. Local game data can generally be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. Feedback, bug-report, review-access, and optional tester-theme submission records may be kept as long as reasonably necessary to investigate issues, improve the game, evaluate submitted themes, prevent abuse, and document review access, then removed when no longer needed. Information processed independently by third-party services is subject to those providers' retention and deletion practices.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. Platform, advertising, and third-party services may apply additional protections where required by law or platform policy.

Security

Online submissions use HTTPS, and private service credentials are stored on the server side rather than inside the Android application. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game, its features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The last-updated date on this page will be revised when changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information shown on the game's Google Play listing.

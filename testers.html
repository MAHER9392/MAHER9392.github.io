What is Tic Tac Toe/XO - The Clean One?

It is a clean, lightweight Tic Tac Toe/XO game built around smooth play, fair challenge, customization, and replayability rather than clutter.

More ways to playClassic, Endless, Local 2 Player, and boards from 3×3 to 6×6.

Change modes, board sizes, languages, themes, and settings. Move between screens. Report freezes, unexpected resets, visual problems, or anything that behaves differently than expected.

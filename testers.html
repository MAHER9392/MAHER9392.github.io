CLOSED TEST · TIC TAC TOE XO

Thanks for testing The Clean One.

I do not need you to play perfectly. I need you to use the game normally, try things, notice what feels good or confusing, and tell me what you would change.

Before you start: Make sure you are using the latest Closed Test version from Google Play. If Google Play shows an Update button, update first.
Join / Open Closed Test Open on Google Play

What I want you to do

1

Play normally

Try several rounds. Test different AI difficulties, Classic or Endless mode, local 2-player if possible, and more than one board size.

2

Create your own theme

Open the Theme Mixer, make a combination you actually like, give the theme a name, then use Send Theme. Please send at least one theme. If sending fails, tell me exactly what happened.

3

Try to break things

Change modes, board sizes, language, themes and settings. Go back and forth between screens. If something looks wrong, freezes, resets unexpectedly, or behaves differently than you expected, report it.

4

Give me your real opinion

Tell me what you liked, what you disliked, what was confusing, what felt unnecessary, and what you think is missing. Suggestions are welcome even if they are small.

5

Rate the experience

Give the current test version a score from 1 to 5 and tell me the main reason for your score. Please judge the game as it is now, not based on features you hope may be added later.

Things worth checking

AI difficultyDoes each level feel different and fair?
ThemesAre colors readable and pleasant?
Menus and controlsIs anything hard to find or understand?
Different board sizesDoes the layout stay clean from 3×3 to 6×6?
LanguageLook for awkward text, clipping, or RTL issues.
Return experienceWould you open the game again later? Why or why not?

How to report feedback

Use the feedback / bug-report option inside the game when possible. For a bug, include what you were doing immediately before it happened. Screenshots are very helpful.

Please do not share this temporary tester page publicly. It is intended for people participating in the Closed Test.

Thank you. Useful criticism helps more than polite praise.

What is Tic Tac Toe/XO - The Clean One?

It is a clean, lightweight Tic Tac Toe/XO game built around smooth play, fair challenge, customization, and replayability rather than clutter.

Adaptive AIFive difficulty levels.
More ways to playClassic, Endless, Local 2 Player, and boards from 3×3 to 6×6.
Theme MixerBuild and test your own visual combinations.
Offline friendlyCore gameplay works offline.
Clean ad approachNo ads interrupt active moves during a match.
LanguagesEnglish, Arabic, and Turkish.

What I want you to test

1

Play normally

Play several rounds and try different AI difficulties, Classic and Endless, Local 2 Player if possible, and more than one board size.

2

Create, name, and send a theme

Open Theme Mixer, create a combination you genuinely like, give it a name, and send it through the tester theme submission. Please send at least one theme.

3

Look for bugs

Change modes, board sizes, languages, themes, and settings. Move between screens. Report freezes, unexpected resets, visual problems, or anything that behaves differently than expected.

4

Give your real opinion

Tell me what you liked, disliked, found confusing, found unnecessary, or think is missing. Small suggestions are useful too.

5

Rate the current version

Give Tic Tac Toe/XO - The Clean One a score from 1 to 5 and tell me the main reason for your score.
